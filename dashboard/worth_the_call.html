Worth The Call

Every one of the 45,211 people this bank's telemarketing campaign contacted, grouped by engagement history into four segments — and how far a call actually went with each one.

Customers who said yes to a previous campaign were 7.1× more likely to subscribe than customers never contacted before (64.7% vs. 9.2%) — yet they received only 2.2% of all contacts made this campaign.

Data: UCI Bank Marketing dataset, 45,211 contacts. Segments built with K-means clustering on engagement history (recency, frequency, prior outcome) — see the project notebooks for method and full write-up. "Efficiency index" = a segment's share of all subscribers gained, divided by its share of all contacts made; above 1 means the segment returned more than its share of effort spent on it.
